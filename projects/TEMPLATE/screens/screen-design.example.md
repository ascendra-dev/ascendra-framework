### 6. Density & Judgment Resolution

| Date | Finding | PO Response | Detail |
|------|---------|-------------|--------|
| 2026-07-14 | Staff App "Standing session" cell (auth-flow test) | Confirmed as-is | Both Finance Manager and Account Owner log in through the same ordinary email/password session — there is no SSO, magic-link, or delegated-access flow for either role (BRD Section 6.3, Delegated Access, is not populated for this project). "Standing session" accurately describes one shared mechanism, not two masked ones. This is the simple baseline case the chapter itself flags Harborview as — no auth-flow split to force a division. |
| 2026-07-14 | SCR-002/SCR-004 `closest:` citations lacking a stated reason | Revised | Added a one-line reason to the Notes column for both rows: SCR-002 explains no exact catalog entry exists for a compact Payer-creation form and names Customer Profile as the nearest real match; SCR-004 explains the same for a multi-section invoice-with-line-items form against Create Product Listing. Both are genuinely novel shapes for this catalog, not a case of the AI stopping early — worth stating plainly rather than leaving the reader to guess which case applies. |
| 2026-07-14 | SCR-002 Route "overlay on SCR-001" phrasing | Acknowledged tradeoff | Kept as-is — the phrasing is necessary information, since SCR-002 has no route of its own in the app's real URL structure and a reader needs to know what it overlays. Understood that this doesn't imply lesser status: SCR-002 still counts as a full, independent Screen Inventory row for sizing and review purposes, same as SCR-004 (Page). The equal-weight rule governs how a screen is *counted*, not how its Route column is worded. |
